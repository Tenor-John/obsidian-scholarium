# Deep Research

Run `scripts/synthesize_gaps.py <workspace> <json-input-or-file>`.

## Boundaries

- Only synthesize from deduped records and evidence cards.
- Separate established evidence, indirect leads, and unknowns.
- Do not invent unread full-text details.
